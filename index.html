HerPhase

HerPhase uses your logged cycle timing and optional wellness check-ins to provide estimated phase and planning context. The current free-first build keeps data on device and does not use accounts, analytics, cloud sync, or tracking.

Release status

The free-first iPhone build is being prepared for the App Store. This site will link to the verified listing after the app is publicly available.

Privacy Terms Support

Private by default

Cycle settings, daily check-ins, moods, physical tags, and notes stay on the device in the free-first version.

Built for planning

HerPhase maps your logged cycle day to an estimated phase and uses optional sleep, stress, and energy check-ins to provide general daily context.

Wellness only

HerPhase is not medical advice, diagnosis, contraception, or a fertility tool. It provides general wellness information.
