Privacy Policy

HerPhase is designed as a private, on-device cycle and energy planner.

Data Collection

HerPhase stores cycle settings, energy levels, mood tags, physical tags, notes, and preferences locally on your device. HerPhase does not require an account, and the app itself does not transmit that cycle and check-in content to a developer-operated server, Apple, or RevenueCat. Your device backup settings may separately include app data.

Analytics and Tracking

HerPhase does not include advertising, use the advertising identifier, or track activity across other companies' apps or websites. RevenueCat provides purchase-history analytics limited to operating and evaluating the subscription service; it does not receive your cycle dates, check-ins, tags, or notes.

Health Data

HerPhase may display general wellness information. It does not collect, store, or transmit HealthKit data.

Notifications

HerPhase may send local notifications for daily check-in reminders and phase change alerts if you enable these features in Settings.

Subscriptions

If you open or use HerPhase Premium purchase features, Apple processes the transaction and RevenueCat helps load products, validate purchases, restore access, and determine whether the Premium entitlement is active. RevenueCat receives an automatically generated anonymous app user ID, purchase and subscription information, and limited technical information such as device type, operating system, locale, and network information. HerPhase does not send RevenueCat your name, email address, cycle dates, check-ins, tags, or notes.

Purchase history is used for app functionality and subscription analytics. It is not linked by HerPhase to a named account and is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking. Apple's handling of App Store transactions is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy; RevenueCat's handling of subscription data is governed by RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Retention and Deletion

Local cycle and check-in data remains on your device until you delete it in HerPhase Settings or remove the app. Purchase records are retained by Apple and RevenueCat under their policies and as needed to validate purchases, restore access, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. To request deletion of subscription data controlled by the developer, email gus@gusdigitalsolutions.com. Deleting app data or uninstalling HerPhase does not cancel an active subscription; follow Apple's subscription cancellation instructions.

Children's Privacy

HerPhase is not intended for use by children under the age of 13.

Contact
