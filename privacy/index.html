Privacy Policy

HerPhase is designed as a private, on-device cycle and energy planner.

Data Collection

HerPhase stores cycle settings, energy levels, mood tags, physical tags, notes, and preferences locally on your device. No personal data is collected, transmitted, or shared with third parties in the free-first submission build.

Analytics and Tracking

No analytics or tracking SDKs are used in the free-first submission build. No behavior data is collected or transmitted.

Health Data

HerPhase may display general wellness information. It does not collect, store, or transmit HealthKit data.

Notifications

HerPhase may send local notifications for daily check-in reminders and phase change alerts if you enable these features in Settings.

Subscriptions

The free-first submission build does not configure RevenueCat or present purchase/restore flows. If subscriptions are enabled in a later version, App Store and RevenueCat subscription disclosures will be updated before release.

Children's Privacy

HerPhase is not intended for use by children under the age of 13.
