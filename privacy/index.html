Privacy Policy

HerPhase is a private, on-device cycle and energy planner. Your entries never leave your phone through the app.

Who provides HerPhase

HerPhase is published by Gustavo Payano Vasquez, doing business as Gus Digital Solutions — a sole proprietorship, not a company. Privacy questions: gus@gusdigitalsolutions.com. This policy describes what the shipping app actually does.

Data Collection

HerPhase stores cycle settings, period start and end dates, energy levels, mood tags, physical tags, notes, and preferences locally on your device. There is no HerPhase account, no sign-up, and no HerPhase server. The app does not send this information to us or to anyone else, and it makes no network request to any server we operate.

This information is sensitive. We designed the app so that we never receive it in the first place.

Backups — the one copy we cannot control

Because your entries live on your iPhone, they are included in your iPhone backups. If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup, a copy of your HerPhase data exists in that backup, under your Apple Account and Apple’s terms. Gus Digital Solutions has no access to your backups. If you would rather no copy leave your phone at all, you can turn off backup for HerPhase in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Backups.

Analytics and Tracking

No analytics, attribution, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK is active in this build. No behavior data is collected or transmitted. HerPhase does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across other apps or websites. Apple may separately provide us crash reports or aggregate diagnostics if you enabled sharing in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements; that is controlled by you, not by the app.

Health Data

HerPhase does not use Apple’s HealthKit framework at all. It does not read from or write to Apple Health, and it does not request Health permission. The cycle and symptom information you type stays in the app’s own local storage as described above.

We are not a HIPAA covered entity

People often assume that anything health-related is covered by HIPAA. It usually is not. HIPAA generally applies to health-care providers, health plans, and their business associates — not to a consumer wellness app you download yourself. HerPhase is not a HIPAA covered entity and this policy is not a HIPAA notice. Other consumer-protection, health-privacy, and breach-notification laws can still apply to us, and we follow them.

HerPhase is also not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition, and it is not contraception or a fertility tool. See our medical disclosure.

Notifications

HerPhase may send local notifications for daily check-in reminders and phase-change alerts if you enable them in Settings. These are scheduled on your device. No server is involved, no push token is created, and no data is transmitted to deliver them.

Subscriptions

The free-first submission build does not configure RevenueCat and does not present purchase or restore flows. The RevenueCat library is linked into the app but is never initialized, so it receives nothing. If subscriptions are enabled in a later version, App Store and RevenueCat disclosures will be updated before that release ships.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because we never receive your data, we hold nothing to retain and there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. Your entries remain on your device until you delete them in the app or delete the app, which removes all of them. Your own device backups are governed by Apple and by your backup settings.

See the delete-your-data instructions for step-by-step guidance.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information a business holds about you, to withdraw consent, to limit the use of sensitive personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. These include the GDPR and UK GDPR, the California CCPA/CPRA, and consumer-health-data laws such as Washington’s My Health My Data Act and Nevada SB 370.

Because HerPhase has no account and no server, in practice we hold no personal information about you and there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete. We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not disclose consumer health data to anyone. If you have emailed us, write to gus@gusdigitalsolutions.com and we will handle your request.

Support Email

If you choose to email us for support, we receive the email address and the message you send, so we can reply. We keep support emails only as long as needed to handle the request and for routine record-keeping, then delete them. We never combine them with app data, because we do not have your app data.

Children’s Privacy

HerPhase is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided us with personal information, email gus@gusdigitalsolutions.com.

Changes to This Policy

If HerPhase’s data practices change — including enabling subscriptions, cloud sync, analytics, or Apple Health — this policy will be updated before the changed version is released, and the effective date at the top will change.
