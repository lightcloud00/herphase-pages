On-device data deletion

Delete Your HerPhase Data

The free-first HerPhase build does not create a cloud account. Your cycle settings, check-ins, tags, notes, and preferences are stored on your device and can be deleted inside the app.

How to delete all your data

  1. Open HerPhase.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Data section.
  4. Tap Delete My Data.
  5. Confirm the alert.

This permanently erases the cycle settings, check-ins, mood and physical tags, notes, and preferences stored by HerPhase on this device. The action cannot be undone.

Accounts and purchases

There is no HerPhase account to close in the free-first build, and the current build does not present purchase, restore, or subscription flows.

Need help?

If you cannot find the Delete My Data button, or you have questions about what gets erased, contact gus@gusdigitalsolutions.com and include your device model, iOS version, and HerPhase app version.
