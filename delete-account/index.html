Account deletion

Delete Your HerPhase Account

HerPhase is local-first: your check-ins, journal entries, mood logs, and planned drinks live only on this device. There is no HerPhase cloud account to delete. Deleting your data is a single action inside the app.

How to delete all your data

  1. Open HerPhase.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the Data section.
  4. Tap Delete My Data.
  5. Confirm the alert.

This permanently erases every check-in, mood entry, journal note, planned drink, and self-care record stored on this device. The action cannot be undone.

What about my subscription?

Deleting your in-app data does not cancel a HerPhase Plus subscription. To cancel your subscription, use the standard iOS Subscriptions screen:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions.
  3. Select HerPhase Plus.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription.

After cancellation, your Plus features remain active until the end of the current billing period.

What about the App Store subscription receipt?

Apple retains your subscription purchase history independent of HerPhase. Your purchase record stays on Apple's servers per App Store policy; deleting your in-app data does not delete that record.
