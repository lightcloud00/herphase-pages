Important wellness disclaimer

Not a Medical Device

HerPhase is a general wellness and journaling app. It is NOT a medical device and does NOT diagnose, treat, cure, or mitigate any disease or condition.

What HerPhase is NOT

  • Not a medical device
  • Not a diagnostic tool
  • Not a fertility or contraception tracker
  • Not a substitute for a healthcare professional
  • Not intended to inform medication, treatment, or clinical decisions

What HerPhase IS

  • A cycle phase estimator based on your logged data
  • An observational wellness journal for mood, energy, sleep, and physical sensations
  • A pattern tracker that surfaces trends from your own check-ins
  • A private, on-device app in the current free-first build
  • A general wellness tool, designed conservatively

Privacy boundary

  • No analytics, no crash reporting SDKs
  • No accounts, no cloud sync, no server transmission
  • All cycle settings, check-ins, and notes stay on this device
  • No third parties receive any of your data

If you experience severe pain, abnormal bleeding, sudden changes in your cycle, or any symptom that worries you, contact a qualified healthcare professional promptly. Do not rely on HerPhase to evaluate symptoms.

When to consult a clinician

Contact a qualified healthcare professional if you have symptoms or changes that concern you, or if you need guidance about contraception, fertility, medication, treatment, or any other medical decision.
